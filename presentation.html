PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Global Food Waste & Hunger Paradox

Massive commercial waste occurs while hunger persists due to lack of coordination and binary disposal models.

1.05 Billion T
Global Food Wasted Yearly

19% of all food available to consumers is discarded annually while 783 million people suffer from chronic hunger (UNEP 2024).

8 – 10% GHG
Methane & Carbon Footprint

Rotting landfill food generates lethal methane emissions (CH4), carrying 28x greater global warming potential than CO2.

$1 Trillion
Annual Economic Loss

Loss of raw materials, clean water, agricultural effort, and merchant revenue caused by fragmented logistics.

Key Systemic Failures

Information Disconnection: Donors lack instant, automated matching channels to notify nearby NGOs before perishable items expire.
Binary Disposal Mindset: Unsold meals are either sold at 100% price or dumped directly in bins, missing dynamic flash-sale recovery.
Zero Cascade Recovery: No automated pipeline exists to sort inedible fractions into animal feed, biomethane energy, or composting.
PROPOSED SOLUTION & INNOVATION

4-Tier Circular Bioeconomy Platform

Foodzyra dynamically matches every fraction of food to its highest and best circular use (US EPA Scale Aligned).

Tier 1: Redistribute

Edible Surplus to NGOs

Instant automated routing of prepared meals from restaurants & caterers to verified food banks, orphanages & shelters.

✓ 0% Waste • 100% Free
Tier 2: Recover

Dynamic Flash Market

Commercial near-expiry items offered at dynamic discounts (50–70% off) to students and budget-conscious buyers.

✓ Algorithmic Pricing
Tier 3: Repurpose

Animal Feed Network

Safe vegetable trimmings, peels, and bakery leftovers diverted directly to local dairy farms and animal sanctuaries.

✓ Feed Security
Tier 4: Regenerate

Biogas & Composting

Spoiled inedible organic matter directed to biomethanation plants for clean power and organic nutrient soil compost.

✓ Renewable Energy

ALGORITHMIC FORMULATIONS & MATHEMATICAL MODELS

Core Algorithmic Engines

Mathematical precision driving proximity dispatch, nutritional mapping, and dynamic markdown pricing.

1. Haversine Proximity & Dispatch Matching Algorithm

Geodesic Distance Engine
d = 2R · arcsin(√(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁)cos(φ₂)sin²(Δλ/2)))
Priority Score = w₁·(1 - d/d_max) + w₂·(1/(T_exp - T_now)) + w₃·U_capacity
Computes spherical great-circle distance between donor GPS and NGOs within a 5 km geofence. Weight matrix ($w_1, w_2, w_3$) prioritizes urgent perishables for immediate pickup.

2. Decoupled 2-Stage Vision-to-USDA Pipeline

Deterministic Grounding
Stage 1 (Perception): Vision Model → Detected Dish Class + Plate %
Stage 2 (Nutrition): Macro_val = Gram_weight · (USDA_Item_Ref / 100g)
Decouples visual segmentation from numerical computation. Guarantees 0% LLM arithmetic hallucination by pulling exact calories, proteins, and carbohydrates from USDA Standard Reference.

3. Dynamic Decay & Markdown Pricing Algorithm

Dynamic Pricing Curve
Discount D(t) = D_min + (D_max - D_min) · (1 - t_remaining / T_shelf_life)^γ
Dynamically increases discounts as closing hour nears ($\gamma > 1$), triggering high-velocity clearance before food turns non-commercial while preserving merchant recovery revenue.

FEASIBILITY & RISK MITIGATION

Operational Viability & Safety Safeguards

Designed for rapid adoption across college campuses, food courts, hotels, and municipal hunger shelters.

Platform Feasibility

Zero-Friction Web/PWA Access: No heavy downloads or app-store gatekeepers. Loads instantly on any smartphone with <2 MB bundle size.
Offline-First Resilience: Service worker caching allows restaurant staff to draft food listings even in basement kitchens without signal.
Modular Scalability: Lightweight micro-architecture easily scales from university campuses to entire municipal food networks.

Risk Mitigation & Safety

Food Safety & Liability: Mandatory prep timestamps, strict perishable expiry windows, and alignment with Good Samaritan legal protections.
Last-Mile Transit Delays: Geofenced dispatch triggers pings only to verified volunteers within an optimal 3–5 km radius.
Quality Verification: Dual-party cryptographic 6-digit claim tokens ensure food handovers are verified before ledger updates.
